01

Research

Biological tissues continuously transport water, nutrients, signalling molecules and cells. The physical mechanisms that control these movements emerge from a subtle interplay between tissue architecture, pressure, deformation and active biological processes.

My work combines mechanics, fluid physics and quantitative biology to uncover the principles linking structure and transport function in living matter.
